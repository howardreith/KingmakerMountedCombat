# Phase 3H targeted native playtest

Private **0.1.0-phase3h-preview.6**. This is a partial candidate: TB full attacks and complete movement-resource reconciliation remain blocked, and visible Horse Bite needs confirmation. One active mounted pair; rider and Horse still have separate native turns. The diagnostic overlay and both experimental turn settings must remain off. Current saddle art is deliberately unchanged.

Use a disposable test save and normal controls. Close Kingmaker normally before any authorized guarded installation. No UMM window or diagnostic commands are needed during play. Record what happened and the weapon/actor/turn; no need to repeat the earlier full review.

1. **Approach first.** Mount the Horse, click a distant visible enemy with a melee rider, and let the order run. Expect Horse-owned approach, stopping at legal reach, then rider attacks. Repeat with party selection. In TB, select the Horse on its own turn and click a distant enemy; it should approach and attack. Explicit Mount Primary should also approach in RTWP. A wall must still prevent an illegal attack.
2. **Ordinary full attacks.** With a longbow and fresh actions, remain stationary and compare Rapid Shot off/on against a surviving target. Compare with the same actor unmounted. Expect the native plan, timing and penalties; extra attacks depend on BAB and other effects. Explicit Rider Primary remains one attack. **Known TB gate:** automation still obtained only one ordinary longbow shot; report the native cursor/action prediction and whether the full-round option changes this. Do not count one shot as a Rapid Shot pass.
3. **Continuation and cancellation.** Leave one RTWP hostile click alone, then try repeated clicks on the same target. Expect completed sequences without starvation or extra instant attacks. Stop, ground movement, a different target and explicit Primary must replace the old intent. An already released arrow may still resolve.
4. **Native turns and costs.** Rider longbow/melee act on the rider turn; Horse Bite acts on the Horse turn. Check each actor's own action display. Try partial Horse movement followed by rider-issued movement and note actual distance plus remaining actions. A second move can be legal when the Horse's Standard is unused. **The complete shared mount allowance is unqualified; this candidate must not be treated as TB-complete.**
5. **Visible Bite.** Watch an unmounted Horse attack, then mounted ordinary and explicit attacks from a clear side view. Expect a visible strike and recovery, followed by idle/next legal action. Damage text alone is insufficient. A short clip is more useful than another static screenshot.
6. **Seat and countdown.** Check front, rear and side views while idle, walking, turning, biting and making rider attacks. The rider is another 0.08 m lower and shifted toward the Horse's anatomical right to center the pelvis; earlier fore/aft placement is retained. Look for clipping or drift. Expect the attack countdown to use an attack icon and the correct action cost. Mount/Dismount tooltips should have no literal `<null>` fields or automatic-use advice.
7. **Preserved controls.** Pause while idle, queue Mount, then unpause: approach/mount should execute once. Stop should cancel a queued Mount. Check paused Dismount, order replacement, party control, dismount and subsequent unmounted longbow/melee attacks.
8. **Mode and lifecycle boundaries.** On the disposable save, check RTWP -> TB -> RTWP, Stop and dismount/remount for stale attacks or replenished actions. Dismount and save/load: no stale visual/navigation lease or automatic remount should remain. This small check does not certify the full save/load matrix or uninstall safety of persistent Horse companion content.
