## Required compromises and controls

- one Medium rider and one exact active rank-7+ Mammoth only;
- out-of-combat movement diagnostic only, default off, no persistence or automatic remount;
- clean dismount at combat, mode, save/load, and area boundaries;
- no Wrath assets/code/assembly, no other-mod dependency, no broad global movement patch;
- two fresh-process passes for every core lifecycle/movement scenario and exact restoration after every run.

Engineering bands after fixture admission: fixture guard/scenario host 2–4 focused engineering days; movement/lifecycle qualification and fixes 5–10 days; visual calibration/manual review 2–5 days. These are planning bands, not a delivery commitment.

Explicit non-goals remain full mounted combat, Cavalier, feats, charge, AoO/reach, ranged/spellcasting, enemy riders, multiple mounts/sizes, polymorph, broad indoor support, persistence, and public release.
