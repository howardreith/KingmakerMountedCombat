## 1. Rider sits too high; dismount/remount improves it

**Primary path:** `Integration/KingmakerMountedPairRuntime.cs` — `AcquireMovementAuthority` (459), `AttachPresentation` (499–564), `RestorePresentation` (577–713), `RestoreMovementAuthority` (727 onward). Attachment finds the profile's live source bone, samples its current world position, creates a root-parented `KMC_RiderPositionAnchor`, acquires a transform lease, configures the rider movement proxy, and creates a pose adapter. The Horse profile uses `Chest` in `Integration/SupportedMountedProfile.cs:69–72`.

**Pose path:** `Integration/MountedRiderPoseAdapter.cs` — `Configure` (100), `ConfigureAnimatedSaddle` (154–173), `Update` (217), `LateUpdate` (234), `ApplyPose` (259). `Domain/AnimatedSaddlePosition.cs:24–42` calibrates `seatFromSource = posedPelvis - source` once and projects the current animated source plus that retained offset/correction. `Domain/ScopedPoseBaselineLease.cs` restores the prior frame before applying the next pose. Dismount deconfigures the pose and releases the attachment before restoring native movement.

**Mechanical versus visual position:** `Integration/RiderMovementAgent.cs:294–310,380–441` synchronizes rider view/root/entity coordinates to the fixed anchor; the late pose adapter then changes bones. The two can disagree visually even when movement telemetry is healthy. Do not infer range errors or cumulative writes solely from the screenshot.

**Investigate:** compare first attachment, mounted-save load, and the improved remount. Record exact rider/mount/view/anchor identity, source and pelvis positions in mount-root coordinates at calibration, retained seat offset, source animation pose, root scale, rider profile, component count and frame-baseline restoration. A one-time calibration from an incompletely settled or differently posed view is a candidate; duplicate components, stale view identity or failed baseline restoration are different candidates. Existing frame-restoration code means “the offset accumulates every frame” is not an established diagnosis. A permanent lower Y constant could conceal the actual lifecycle bug.

**Existing targeted tests:** `tests/MountedRiderPoseTests.cs` has nonaccumulation, frame priming and restoration cases (105–244). `tests/MountedStockAttackPolicyTests.cs:404–436` covers animated-seat projection and replacement calibration. `tests/PostLoadViewReadinessTests.cs` is the relevant restore-readiness family. These tests do not substitute for final-render observation.

## 2. Nearby enemy click does not approach/attack in turn-based mode

**Input/intent path:** `Integration/MountedCombatController.cs` — `ObserveStockAttackRequested` (665), `TryRouteMountedStockAttack` (1034–1168), `TryDriveStockAttackIntent` (1393–1564), `TryExecuteAction` (824–932). The native click is bound to its exact command; the pair controller consumes it and submits a `MountedPairAttackCommand` to the acting actor. Native cannot-attack clicks that produce a point move have a separate exact router in `Integration/MountedClickApproachTranspiler.cs`; investigate that only if the actual click took that branch.

**Approach path:** `Integration/MountedPairAttackCommand.cs` — `OnStart` (853–902), `TickApproach` (1058–1200), `BeginDelegatedMove` (1287–1428), `StartChildAttack` (1493–1541). The wrapper determines native admission and, when needed, submits a `UnitMoveTo` into the mount's Move slot. It stops the exact carrier at legal range, then begins the native attack plan. Preserve that ownership/cleanup boundary.

**Geometry path:** `Integration/MountedPairSingleAttack.cs` — `TryCalculatePairStoppingRadius` (110), `EvaluateCurrentNativeAdmission` (181), `TryPrepareNativeStartAdmission` (232), `EvaluateExactNativeGate` (289), `TryObserveDistances` (323). Rider melee uses mount-origin mechanics distance, mount/target corpulence, and the current planned weapon's range, then checks the native executor-distance and line-of-sight gates. `Domain/MountedCombatSpatialPolicy.cs:160–193` bounds executor-radius compensation to 0.75 units and derives delegated move radius. Do not globally enlarge reach or remove LOS as a workaround.

**Active turn authority:** default `Diagnostics/DiagnosticSettings.cs:5–33` enables paired activation and disables the two legacy experiments. The important current methods are `Integration/PairedActivationLifecycle.cs:75–99` (`CanAddressActor`, `CanMovePairedMount`), `Integration/UnifiedMountedTurnCoordinator.cs:631–650` (`TickMountMovement`), and `Integration/PairedActivationControls.cs` for movement input/projection. Do not repair only the retired paired scheduler and assume the owner's default is covered.

**Capture for the reported click:** command origin and actor, activation identity/phase, actor Standard/Move debt, mount granted movement, five-foot-step/single-move settings, target/body/weapon distances, LOS result, native-admission state, Move-slot and queue ownership, and the first reason approach failed or waited. The supplied TB screenshot reportedly shows “Move 1”; that is insufficient evidence for a free step or full attack. Compare a fresh eligible turn, a partly spent turn, and a legitimately exhausted turn.

## 3. Does the horse attack when eligible?

`Domain/MountedStockAttack.cs:140–179` chooses the next ready actor. RT can dispatch both in succession; under the default current TB setup, an ordinary rider click is not blanket authority for the mount to spend an action. `MountedCombatController.cs:1456–1470` passes each actor's legality/readiness into that policy; `CaptureContext` (2139–2236) rechecks native turn, capability, target and Standard action availability. `PairedActivationLifecycle` maintains each actor's granted/spent/ended state.

The owner's desired behavior is a **pair-level ordinary melee order**: retain that enemy and let an eligible horse follow through without requiring a redundant second click. This is a bounded change to the current TB input contract, not proof that today's horse dispatcher is losing every eligible order. The current controller passes `mountIsLegalActor = !turnBased || stockIntent.MountActor || settings.UseLegacyUnifiedTurn`, so a default paired-activation rider click deliberately excludes the mount. `tests/MountedStockAttackPolicyTests.cs:64–80` even asserts that an actor-specific request does not consume its ready partner's action. Preserve that actor-specific meaning for explicit per-actor orders/overrides; add the pair-order behavior in the correct ordinary-input scope.

For actual execution, `Integration/NativeSingleAttackWeaponResolver.cs` resolves the native primary natural weapon. `MountedPairAttackCommand.CreateAndValidateChildAttack` (1543–1594) checks the exact slot/weapon and permits an ordinary native sequence when the click is ordinary (`TryExecuteAction` passes `singleAttack = !ordinaryIntent`). The resulting action remains `MountPrimaryNatural`; the enum name alone does not mean every ordinary routine is forcibly one attack.

**Investigate:** distinguish “mount never received an eligible order,” “order refused for a legitimate spent/ended/condition state,” “order admitted but no native roll,” and “roll occurred with weak visual feedback.” Bind horse attack rules to the horse's actor ID and weapon/target; do not infer participation from rider damage or any nearby hoof animation. Preserve lawful single/full-attack planning and each actor's independent resources. At every follow-through dispatch, use actual `CanAddressActor` plus the horse's readiness, native conditions, range/LOS and retained-order ownership; merely setting a blanket `mountIsLegalActor = true` is insufficient.

**Recommended requirement:** “With default paired activation, one ordinary mounted melee attack order retains the target for the pair. After the pair approaches as needed, the rider and horse each perform the native attack or routine they are legally entitled to during that paired activation, without a redundant mount attack click. Dispatch may be sequential. The horse acts only after its own grant/preparation and while it remains addressable, unended, capable and appropriately resourced; explicit per-actor orders, cancellation, target changes and End Turn override the retained pair order. Do not grant or reset an action, movement, initiative or cooldown, and do not execute a strike outside the actor's lawful activation.”

The minimum acceptance is within an existing legal paired activation. Do not silently expand this into new cross-round TB auto-end/auto-attack behavior. RT already has persistent intent across cooldowns. `Domain/PairedActivation.cs:73,200–224` defines the open/prepared/unended boundary; mid-encounter adoption explicitly marks an already-used partner ended (`121–143`), which must remain protected.

## 4. RT AI idle at combat start; attack click pauses before movement

**Automatic acquisition:** `MountedCombatController.TryRouteMountedStockAttack` recognizes native `UnitAttack` proposals with `AiAction`; `ObserveAutomaticProposal` (1188–1249) evaluates them through `Domain/MountedAutomaticAttackPolicy.cs:53–130`. This path does not scan or rank targets itself. The native rider brain supplies the target; the mount's autonomous AI is deliberately leased off in `KingmakerMountedPairRuntime.AcquireMovementAuthority` (477–484), so the rider/pair dispatcher is essential.

The automatic gate distinguishes AI off, inactive brain, explicit Stop, player-move Hold, occupied commands/queues, invalid target and action incapacity. Reuse `AutomaticProposalCount`, admitted/refused/continued counts, `LastAutomaticRefusal`, `LastPlayerOperationDetail` and `LastStockAttackObservation`. Determine first whether no proposal arrived, a proposal was refused, or accepted intent did not progress. An explicit completed ground move intentionally creates the native-style Hold; fresh combat-start reproduction should record this state rather than assuming the player never moved.

**Concrete timing hypothesis:** `TryDriveStockAttackIntent` supplies `rider.HasStandardAction()` / `mount.HasStandardAction()` to `MountedStockAttack.DecideNext`; if neither actor is ready, RT returns `Wait`. `MountedCombatActionEvaluator` separately rejects an unavailable Standard action (`Domain/MountedCombatAction.cs:408–412`). Delegated movement is created only after actor dispatch, in wrapper `OnStart`. This is a plausible point where locomotion can wait for attack readiness, consistent with the reported hesitation. It is not yet a proven cause.

Measure input time/frame, intent acceptance, native preparation/initiative, actor cooldowns, wrapper admission/start, carrier creation, first real movement, and first native attack against a comparable unmounted actor. If native locomotion is legal while the attack is not yet ready, remove only the unnecessary mounted movement delay; do not reset initiative/cooldowns or release the strike early. Preserve native pause, Hold/Stop, queued player orders and invalid-target boundaries. Reuse existing `tests/MountedAutomaticAttackPolicyTests.cs`, `tests/MountedStockAttackPolicyTests.cs` and `tests/Finalize4OrdersAiTransitionsTests.cs`, selecting cases affected by the eventual fix.

## 5. Bite damage without bite animation; hoof looks correct

`Integration/HorsePrimaryAttackAnimationAdapter.cs:48–167` validates/adopts the exact stock Horse SpecialAttack handle, or supplies one only when the stock handle is absent. It is presentation-only; native `UnitAttack` owns attacks, timing and damage. Its exact context guard begins at 141.

Ordinary dispatched horse routines do reach this integration: the controller chooses `MountPrimaryNatural`, wrapper `CreateAndValidateChildAttack` calls `SupplyExact`, and `MountedPatchController.AttackAnimationPostfix` (1343–1346) calls `MountedAnimationAdapter.SupplyExactHorsePrimaryAnimation` (128–138) after native handle creation. `MountedCombatController.TryGetExactHorsePrimaryAnimationContext` (339–355) checks the active command and current planned hand. It would be incorrect to diagnose the ordinary path as wholly bypassing the adapter without a trace.

**Investigate:** actual primary weapon and its SpecialAnimation, selected ActionSet/action, handle adoption/supply/rejection, later native handle refresh, playback/interrupt state, animator activity and blending, and whether the visible first strike corresponds to the logged Bite. Existing outcomes expose handle source/action name/acted/finished/interrupted (`MountedPairAttackCommand.cs:1827–1833`); these alone do not prove that a visible bite pose was rendered. Preserve the working hoof animation and native attack count/timing; do not add a second damage event to make the animation obvious.

## Bounded acceptance matrix for the new mission

| Owner issue | Positive outcome to demonstrate | Required boundary / countercase |
|---|---|---|
| High rider | Stable supported rider seating after initial mount, mounted load, and dismount/remount; no growing or retained offset | Normal unmounted pose restored; view/root and bone effects distinguished; no save-layout rewrite to mask it |
| TB nearby click | One ordinary legal click retains its target, advances using the mount when needed, and reaches a legal native attack without an extra ground click | Spent movement/Standard, blocked LOS and impossible path do not grant movement, a free step, reach or attacks |
| Horse participation | One ordinary pair melee order yields rider and horse follow-through when each is eligible within the lawful paired activation, with no redundant mount click and attributable horse combat-log/rule evidence | Sequential execution is allowed; explicit per-actor override/End Turn and own resources/conditions remain authoritative; no new grant, off-turn strike or automatic cross-round expansion |
| RT autoattack | AI-on idle pair automatically acquires the nearest eligible enemy as requested, then approaches/attacks when legal | Define eligibility and distance; preserve explicit Stop/Hold, AI-off, hidden/invalid targets and newer orders; inspect native proposals before adding a second target-selection mechanism |
| RT click responsiveness | Mounted movement starts as promptly as comparable legal unmounted movement; no extra wait solely for attack readiness | The attack still obeys native initiative, cooldown, range and timing; a native restriction is reported, not bypassed |
| Bite presentation | Visible native bite associated with the actual bite strike; hoof still works | Animation handle evidence plus visual owner observation; native damage/count/timing unchanged |
| Existing Mounted Shield | Feat publication/icon and passive shield AC/check behavior retained | No new toggle; no reversion to main-only baseline or loader-pin churn |

Use focused source tests and the actual affected build/package checks, then the owner's alpha playtest under the newly accepted test policy. Static checks can close code-contract regressions; visual/native rows remain explicitly pending until observed. Do not inherit the old full native matrix, historical seed hashes, or broad diagnostic rehabilitation as a fresh release blocker for this mission. Any optional autonomous native attempt still follows the existing safe launch/save/profile ownership rules.
