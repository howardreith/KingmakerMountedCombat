# Targeting, reach, and charge contract

Status: TODO

Combat production is locked until explicit user acceptance of the exact Phase 2A visual-review build.

The first combat seam is pair-scoped: one stationary rider melee attack, then one explicit Mammoth primary natural attack. Targeting must identify the actual attacker and target, derive range from a documented mounted spatial origin/reach contract, and leave every non-mounted unit on stock behavior. Global reach or target replacement is forbidden.

Movement-to-attack must retain the Mammoth as sole pathfinding authority and use attacker-specific stopping distance without oscillation or duplicate commands. Reach, attacks of opportunity, and a basic charge are stretch features only after the core alpha is green. Each may be disabled and recorded `DEFER — EVIDENCED` without invalidating the core unless it fires a core kill criterion.

Ranged attacks and spellcasting while mounted remain explicitly unsupported and must be rejected with a clear reason.
